# GHL Brands — Offer Framework

Internal reference for what the company sells, to whom, and why. This exists
before any individual sales page or pitch — it's the shared definition every
offer underneath GHL Brands should trace back to. Company- and market-level
sections are fixed; each offer section is filled out independently using the
same structure so offers stay comparable and non-overlapping.

---

## 1. Company

**GHL Brands** helps agencies and SaaS companies build, improve, and operate
their HighLevel systems through specialized implementation, support,
engineering, and growth services.

GHL Brands is the parent company. Each numbered offer below (Audit, Onboard,
Help Desk, Engineers, Accelerator, Custom Projects) is a distinct product
sitting underneath this umbrella — sold separately, staffed separately,
priced separately, but all pointing back to the same core promise: a
HighLevel account and business that runs reliably without depending entirely
on one person's memory of how it was built.

Content, video, admin/VA, and chat brands in the broader portfolio (GHL
Social, GHL Sites, GHL Admins, GHL Video, GHL Chat) are separate product
lines with their own offer definitions and are out of scope for this
document — this framework covers the HighLevel operations & engineering
services line only.

## 2. Market

The broader group we sell to:

> Companies using HighLevel to sell, fulfill, support, or manage services.

That market divides into four segments. Not every offer targets every
segment — see each offer's Ideal Buyer section below.

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| HighLevel agencies | Resell or deliver services to their own clients on HighLevel |
| HighLevel SaaS companies | Package HighLevel as their own software product |
| Established businesses using HighLevel internally | Run their own operations on HighLevel, no resale |
| Entrepreneurs building a HighLevel-based product or service | Pre- or early-stage, building from scratch |

## 3. Core Problem Category

Across all four segments, the shared problem is the same shape:

> They need better systems, implementation, support, or technical
> capabilities than they can reliably provide internally.

Every offer below is one specific, scoped answer to one slice of that
problem — never the whole thing at once.

## 4. Service Categories

| Motion | Offer | Buyer stage it targets |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnose | GHL Audit | Unsure what's actually wrong |
| Implement | GHL Onboard | Starting fresh or replatforming |
| Support | GHL Help Desk | Live, but unreliable day-to-day |
| Engineer | GHL Engineers | Live, but needs custom capability |
| Grow & Advise | GHL Accelerator | Stable, but plateaued |
| Build | Custom Projects | Needs something that doesn't exist yet |

Internally: *we diagnose, implement, support, engineer, and improve
HighLevel-based businesses.*

## GHL Audit — Diagnose

**Ideal Buyer**
HighLevel agencies and established internal teams with an active account
that has grown messy over 6+ months of use — multiple builders touching it,
no documentation, and no confidence about what's actually still working.

- **Company type:** Agency or established business with an active HighLevel account
- **Stage:** Established, or rebuilding after a bad implementation
- **Size:** 1+ live subaccounts, real client or customer traffic running through it
- **Current problem:** No one knows what's broken, duplicated, or safe to touch
- **Buying trigger:** A workflow silently failed, a client complained, or a new hire can't make sense of the account
- **Decision-maker:** Founder or operations lead
- **Budget level:** Willing to pay for clarity before committing to a bigger fix
- **Disqualifiers:** Brand-new accounts with nothing built yet (→ GHL Onboard instead)

**Buyer Problem**
Their HighLevel account has become disorganized, automations are unreliable,
and the team does not know what is working. This creates missed leads, slow
fulfillment, and constant dependence on whoever originally built it.

**Desired Result**
We move the buyer from an account of unknown health to a documented,
prioritized list of what's broken, what's risky, and what to fix first.

**Offer**
GHL Audit helps established HighLevel agencies and businesses move from an
account of unknown health to a clear, prioritized fix list, through a
structured technical review, within two weeks.

**Delivery Method**
We produce the result through the **Account Health Review**, which includes:
account and workflow inventory, automation and integration testing,
data/pipeline hygiene check, security and permissions review, and a
findings report with a severity-ranked action plan.

**Scope**
- **Included:** full account walkthrough, automation testing, findings report, 45-minute readout call
- **Not included:** fixing the issues found (quoted separately as Engineers or Help Desk work)
- **Requests submitted:** kickoff form + read-only account access
- **Response/delivery time:** findings report within 10 business days of access being granted
- **Customer responsibilities:** grant account access, join the readout call
- **Service period:** one-time engagement, no recurring commitment
- **Overage rules:** none — fixed-scope, fixed-price

**Commercial Structure**
$997 one-time. Includes one account, up to 3 subaccounts reviewed in depth.
Additional subaccounts +$250 each. No ongoing commitment required.

**Reason to Believe**
HighLevel-only specialization, a repeatable diagnostic checklist built from
hundreds of accounts, and a fixed price that removes the risk of an
open-ended discovery engagement.

**Next Step**
Book an audit intake call.

**Complete Internal Offer Formula**
GHL Brands sells GHL Audit to established HighLevel agencies and businesses
whose accounts have grown disorganized and unreliable. We help them achieve
a clear, prioritized fix list through a structured Account Health Review.
The offer includes a full account walkthrough, automation testing, and a
severity-ranked findings report, takes two weeks, and costs $997 one-time
for up to 3 subaccounts. We are a strong choice because we specialize
exclusively in HighLevel and use a repeatable diagnostic process instead of
open-ended discovery. The next step is an audit intake call.

## GHL Onboard — Implement

**Ideal Buyer**
New HighLevel SaaS companies and entrepreneurs launching a HighLevel-based
product who need a working, sellable system fast and don't have in-house
build capacity yet.

- **Company type:** SaaS company or entrepreneur, sometimes an agency launching a new offer
- **Stage:** New or pre-launch
- **Size:** Pre-revenue to early revenue, small or no internal technical team
- **Current problem:** No working system yet, or a partial build that isn't sellable
- **Buying trigger:** A launch date, a funding milestone, or a signed early customer waiting on delivery
- **Decision-maker:** Founder
- **Budget level:** Willing to invest in a working v1 rather than build it themselves
- **Disqualifiers:** Accounts that already have a working setup and just need fixes (→ GHL Audit or Help Desk)

**Buyer Problem**
They are currently trying to launch on HighLevel with no proven system,
piecing together templates and tutorials on their own time, which is
causing delayed launch dates and a product that isn't ready to put in front
of paying customers.

**Desired Result**
We move the buyer from no working HighLevel system to a live, tested,
sellable setup they can put in front of customers immediately.

**Offer**
GHL Onboard helps new HighLevel SaaS companies and entrepreneurs move from
no working system to a live, launch-ready setup, through a guided
build-and-launch program, within 30 days.

**Delivery Method**
We produce the result through the **Onboard Accelerator**, which includes:
technical discovery, core build (pipelines, automations, funnels),
white-label/branding setup, team walkthrough and training, and a go-live
checklist.

**Scope**
- **Included:** one core build (single product line), training session, 30 days of post-launch support
- **Not included:** custom integrations beyond native HighLevel features (→ Custom Projects), ongoing support after 30 days (→ Help Desk)
- **Requests submitted:** kickoff form, weekly build-review calls
- **Response/delivery time:** live system within 30 days of kickoff
- **Customer responsibilities:** provide brand assets, respond to build-review requests within 3 business days
- **Service period:** one project cycle (30 days) + 30-day support window
- **Overage rules:** scope changes mid-build are quoted as change orders

**Commercial Structure**
$1,997 project fee, paid 50/50 (deposit at kickoff, balance at go-live).
Covers one core build. Additional products or brands scoped separately.

**Reason to Believe**
A named, repeatable build process instead of a custom scope-from-scratch
engagement each time, plus direct experience launching HighLevel SaaS
products for the same buyer profile.

**Next Step**
Complete the onboarding intake form.

**Complete Internal Offer Formula**
GHL Brands sells GHL Onboard to new HighLevel SaaS companies and
entrepreneurs with no working system and a launch date approaching. We help
them achieve a live, sellable HighLevel setup through the Onboard
Accelerator. The offer includes core build, training, and 30 days of
support, takes 30 days, and costs $1,997 split 50/50. We are a strong
choice because we run a repeatable build process rather than starting from
scratch each time. The next step is completing the onboarding intake form.

## GHL Help Desk — Support

**Ideal Buyer**
Established HighLevel agencies with active clients that have outgrown their
current setup and need reliable technical support without hiring another
full-time employee.

- **Company type:** Agency
- **Stage:** Established, growing
- **Size:** Multiple active client subaccounts, small internal team
- **Current problem:** Recurring technical issues with no dedicated person to own them
- **Buying trigger:** A key builder left, ticket backlog is growing, or client complaints are increasing
- **Decision-maker:** Founder or operations lead
- **Budget level:** Prefers a predictable monthly cost over a full-time hire
- **Disqualifiers:** Agencies with zero active clients, or those needing one-off project work only (→ Custom Projects)

**Buyer Problem**
Their HighLevel account has become disorganized, automations are
unreliable, and the team does not know what is working. This creates missed
leads, slow fulfillment, and constant dependence on the owner.

**Desired Result**
We move the buyer from an unreliable HighLevel setup that depends on the
owner to a documented, organized system the team can operate confidently.

**Offer**
GHL Help Desk helps established HighLevel agencies resolve technical
issues, maintain their account, and support their team through ongoing
access to experienced HighLevel specialists.

**Delivery Method**
We provide support through a structured request system that includes issue
diagnosis, troubleshooting, account updates, documentation, and ongoing
maintenance.

**Scope**
- **Included:** unlimited ticket submissions, up to the monthly hour allotment, priority triage
- **Not included:** net-new builds, custom engineering (→ Engineers), strategy consulting (→ Accelerator)
- **Requests submitted:** shared ticketing portal
- **Response/delivery time:** same-business-day response, resolution time depends on ticket complexity
- **Customer responsibilities:** submit tickets with account access and reproduction steps
- **Service period:** month-to-month after a 3-month initial commitment
- **Overage rules:** hours beyond the monthly allotment billed at the standard hourly rate, or rolled into a larger engagement

**Commercial Structure**
$1,500 per month with a three-month initial commitment. Includes up to 20
support hours per month. Additional engineering or project work is scoped
separately.

**Reason to Believe**
A dedicated ticketing system (not ad-hoc Slack access), specialists who work
exclusively in HighLevel, and documentation left behind after every ticket
so knowledge doesn't leave with any one person.

**Next Step**
Schedule an account review call.

**Complete Internal Offer Formula**
GHL Brands sells GHL Help Desk to established HighLevel agencies whose
active clients have outgrown their current setup. We help them achieve a
documented, team-operable system through a structured ticketing process
covering diagnosis, fixes, and maintenance. The offer includes up to 20
support hours per month, runs month-to-month after a 3-month minimum, and
costs $1,500/month. We are a strong choice because our specialists work
exclusively in HighLevel and document every fix instead of leaving
knowledge locked in one person's head. The next step is an account review
call.

## GHL Engineers — Engineer

**Ideal Buyer**
Established agencies and SaaS companies using HighLevel that need custom
technical solutions beyond standard account administration.

- **Company type:** Agency or SaaS company
- **Stage:** Established, scaling
- **Size:** Revenue-generating, technical complexity beyond native HighLevel workflows
- **Current problem:** Needs a custom integration, AI tool, or application HighLevel doesn't natively support
- **Buying trigger:** A client or internal requirement HighLevel's out-of-the-box tools can't meet
- **Decision-maker:** Founder or technical lead
- **Budget level:** Project-based investment, not a recurring retainer
- **Disqualifiers:** Buyers who just need standard automation fixes (→ Help Desk) or a first build (→ Onboard)

**Buyer Problem**
They need functionality — an integration, AI tool, or custom workflow — that
standard HighLevel account administration cannot deliver, which is blocking
a client commitment or an internal capability they've already promised to
build.

**Desired Result**
We move the buyer from a capability gap to a working, tested custom
solution integrated into their existing HighLevel setup.

**Offer**
GHL Engineers helps established agencies and SaaS companies build reliable
integrations, AI tools, applications, and custom workflows through scoped
engineering projects.

**Delivery Method**
Each engagement includes technical discovery, solution design, development,
testing, and documentation.

**Scope**
- **Included:** everything defined in the signed project scope document
- **Not included:** ongoing maintenance after delivery (→ Help Desk retainer), unscoped feature requests (new change order)
- **Requests submitted:** discovery call → written scope document → signed approval before build starts
- **Response/delivery time:** timeline set per project during scoping
- **Customer responsibilities:** provide API/system access, respond to review checkpoints within agreed windows
- **Service period:** project-based, ends at delivery and handoff
- **Overage rules:** anything outside the signed scope is a change order, quoted before work begins

**Commercial Structure**
Projects are priced according to scope and complexity, starting at $2,400.
50% deposit to begin, balance due at delivery. Ongoing maintenance available
separately through GHL Help Desk.

**Reason to Believe**
The team understands both HighLevel and the external systems that must
connect to it — not just HighLevel administration, actual software
engineering applied to the platform.

**Next Step**
Book a technical discovery call.

**Complete Internal Offer Formula**
GHL Brands sells GHL Engineers to established agencies and SaaS companies
using HighLevel that need custom technical solutions beyond standard
account administration. We help them achieve working, reliable
integrations, AI tools, and custom workflows through scoped engineering
projects covering discovery, design, development, testing, and
documentation. Projects start at $2,400, priced to scope and complexity,
with 50% due at kickoff. We are a strong choice because our team
understands both HighLevel and the external systems that must connect to
it. The next step is a technical discovery call.

## GHL Accelerator — Grow & Advise

**Ideal Buyer**
Established HighLevel agencies or SaaS companies with a stable, working
account whose growth has plateaued and who need strategic direction, not
another technical fix.

- **Company type:** Agency or SaaS company
- **Stage:** Established, plateaued — the technical foundation already works
- **Size:** Steady client base or subscriber base with flat or slowing growth
- **Current problem:** The system runs fine but revenue growth has stalled and the team lacks a clear playbook for what to change next
- **Buying trigger:** A growth target has been missed for two or more quarters
- **Decision-maker:** Founder
- **Budget level:** Willing to invest in ongoing strategic guidance, not just one-time fixes
- **Disqualifiers:** Buyers whose account is still technically broken (→ Audit or Help Desk first)

**Buyer Problem**
Their HighLevel account and offers work, but growth has flattened and the
team doesn't have a clear, prioritized plan for what to change next — every
new idea competes for the same limited attention.

**Desired Result**
We move the buyer from a plateaued, technically-sound account to a
prioritized growth plan with a partner accountable for helping execute it.

**Offer**
GHL Accelerator helps established agencies and SaaS companies move past a
growth plateau, through ongoing strategic advisory and execution support
paired with their existing HighLevel setup.

**Delivery Method**
We produce the result through a **quarterly growth cycle**, which includes:
performance review, priority-setting session, execution roadmap, biweekly
advisory calls, and progress tracking against agreed metrics.

**Scope**
- **Included:** quarterly planning session, biweekly advisory calls, progress dashboard
- **Not included:** hands-on build or engineering work (scoped separately through Engineers or Custom Projects)
- **Requests submitted:** shared planning doc, scheduled call cadence
- **Response/delivery time:** biweekly call cadence, async support between calls
- **Customer responsibilities:** attend calls, own execution of agreed action items between sessions
- **Service period:** quarterly cycles, renews by mutual agreement
- **Overage rules:** additional hands-on execution work quoted separately

**Commercial Structure**
$1,200 per month, billed quarterly ($3,600/quarter). Includes biweekly
advisory calls and one quarterly planning session. Execution work is scoped
and billed separately.

**Reason to Believe**
Direct visibility into what's actually working across the GHL Brands
portfolio of agencies and SaaS companies, and a structured quarterly cycle
instead of open-ended consulting calls.

**Next Step**
Schedule a growth plateau assessment call.

**Complete Internal Offer Formula**
GHL Brands sells GHL Accelerator to established HighLevel agencies and SaaS
companies whose growth has plateaued despite a working technical
foundation. We help them achieve a prioritized, executed growth plan
through a quarterly advisory cycle of planning sessions and biweekly calls.
The offer includes quarterly planning and biweekly advisory access, runs in

## Custom Projects — Build

**Ideal Buyer**
Agencies, SaaS companies, or established businesses that need something
built for HighLevel that doesn't fit a standard offer — a new brand's
system from scratch, a large-scale migration, or a bespoke internal tool.

- **Company type:** Agency, SaaS, or established business
- **Stage:** Any — new build, migration, or major expansion
- **Size:** Project scope typically spans multiple subaccounts or a full platform migration
- **Current problem:** A need that doesn't map cleanly onto Audit, Onboard, Help Desk, Engineers, or Accelerator
- **Buying trigger:** A major initiative — new product launch, platform migration, acquisition integration
- **Decision-maker:** Founder, sometimes alongside a technical lead
- **Budget level:** Willing to invest in a larger, project-scoped engagement
- **Disqualifiers:** Requests better served by an existing named offer — those are redirected instead of custom-scoped

**Buyer Problem**
They have a business need tied to HighLevel that doesn't fit any of our
named offers, and going without a dedicated partner risks either building it
wrong or not building it at all.

**Desired Result**
We move the buyer from an unscoped, one-off need to a delivered, working
solution built specifically for their situation.

**Offer**
Custom Projects helps agencies, SaaS companies, and established businesses
get purpose-built HighLevel solutions delivered, through a fully bespoke
scoping and build process, on a timeline set per project.

**Delivery Method**
We produce the result through a **custom scoping process**, which includes:
discovery, proposal and fixed-scope quote, milestone-based build, review
checkpoints, and handoff/documentation.

**Scope**
- **Included:** whatever is defined in the signed proposal
- **Not included:** anything outside the proposal — no scope is assumed
- **Requests submitted:** discovery call → written proposal → signed approval before work begins
- **Response/delivery time:** set per project at proposal stage
- **Customer responsibilities:** provide access and decisions at each review checkpoint
- **Service period:** project-based, defined per proposal
- **Overage rules:** any request outside the signed proposal is a separate change order

**Commercial Structure**
Quoted per project after discovery. Milestone-based payment schedule (typically deposit, midpoint, delivery) defined in the proposal.

**Reason to Believe**
The same specialists behind GHL Audit, Onboard, Help Desk, Engineers, and
Accelerator — meaning a custom project is scoped by people who already know
where HighLevel's edges are, not a generalist dev shop learning the
platform on the client's dime.
